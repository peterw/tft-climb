Do this differently

  • Get Master Yi to 3-star. 3-star averages 3.55, 2-star 5.94.
  • Commit items to Master Yi. With 3 items it averages 4.17; with none, 5.65.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 9 average 2.25 and level 7 averages 5.88. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
  • Scout for Defender Rengar. You place 0.35 worse when it's in your lobby, so if it's there and contesting your units, pivot early.
