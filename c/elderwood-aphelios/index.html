The principle

Play your strongest early board and save. Level to 8 on 4-1 or 4-2 and roll down hard for the 4-cost carries. Keep leveling with the gold you have left.

Typical level timings in this comp: L5 @ 2-5 → L6 @ 3-2 → L7 @ 3-6 → L8 @ 4-2 → L9 @ 5-5 → L10 @ 7-1.

Do this differently

  • Get Aphelios to 3-star. 3-star averages 1.15, 2-star 3.91.
  • Commit items to Aphelios. With 3 items it averages 4.24; with none, 4.95.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 10 average 2.03 and level 7 averages 6.31. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
  • Scout for Executioner Sett. You place 0.26 worse when it's in your lobby, so if it's there and contesting your units, pivot early.
