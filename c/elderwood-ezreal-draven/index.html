The principle

Stabilize at level 8 around 4-2 with your 4-costs. Then save to 9 (or 10) on stage 5 and roll for the 5-costs. Your HP buys that time, so don't bleed it early.

Typical level timings in this comp: L5 @ 2-5 → L6 @ 3-2 → L7 @ 3-5 → L8 @ 4-2 → L9 @ 5-1 → L10 @ 7-1.

Do this differently

  • Get Ezreal to 3-star. 3-star averages 1.47, 2-star 3.81.
  • Commit items to Ezreal. With 3 items it averages 4.16; with none, 4.29.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 10 average 1.94 and level 8 averages 6.64. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
  • Scout for Defender Rengar. You place 0.10 worse when it's in your lobby, so if it's there and contesting your units, pivot early.
