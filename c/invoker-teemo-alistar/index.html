Do this differently

  • Get Teemo to 3-star. 3-star averages 3.96, 2-star 6.21.
  • Commit items to Teemo. With 3 items it averages 4.13; with none, 5.27.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 10 average 1.92 and level 6 averages 7.31. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
