Do this differently

  • Get Kayle to 3-star. 3-star averages 3.94, 2-star 5.62.
  • Commit items to Kayle. With 3 items it averages 4.17; with none, 4.95.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 9 average 2.58 and level 6 averages 6.75. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
  • Scout for Elderwood Kayle. You place 0.49 worse when it's in your lobby, so if it's there and contesting your units, pivot early.
