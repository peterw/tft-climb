The principle

Hit level 7 around 3-5 or 4-1. Roll down for 3-star 3-costs, keeping 50 gold if you can, then level to 8 once the carry is 3-star.

Do this differently

  • Get Mama Beak to 3-star. 3-star averages 2.88, 2-star 4.60.
  • Commit items to Mama Beak. With 3 items it averages 3.42; with none, 4.68.
  • Keep leveling. Games that end at level 10 average 2.02 and level 7 averages 5.58. Part of that is just survival, but don't sit on gold at 8.
  • Scout for Rapidfire Aphelios. You place 0.34 worse when it's in your lobby, so if it's there and contesting your units, pivot early.
