Decisions

HP is a currency. Spend it on purpose.

Lose streak or win streak, not 50/50.

The principle

Each streak pays bonus gold. Alternating wins and losses pays nothing and still costs HP. Choose early: lose on purpose with a weak board and bank gold, or stay strong and win. The worst spot is in between.

A real example (live data)

Fast 9 comps this patch (Vanguard Alune, Vanguard Morgana, Elderwood Ezreal Draven) only work if you reach stage 5 with enough HP to keep saving. That's why the early game matters even when you plan to lose it.

Do this instead

  • After 2-1, decide: streak up (buy units, slam items) or lose streak (sell to 50, take the fights you lose).
  • With about 40 HP or less, stop saving and roll to stabilize.
  • Track your HP compared with the lobby, not on its own. Being 4th-highest at 30 HP is fine.
Don't: Don't roll for upgrades at level 6 with 70 HP and no plan.
