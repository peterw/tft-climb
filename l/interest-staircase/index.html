Econ

Interest is a staircase, and each step is 10 gold

Spend down to a breakpoint, not below it.

The principle

You earn 1 bonus gold for every 10 gold you hold at the end of a round, up to 5 at 50. Sitting at 50 by early stage 3 roughly doubles your income compared with spending every turn, and that pays for your level 8 roll-down.

A real example (live data)

Say you're at 47 gold. Rolling once (2 gold) leaves 45: you still get 4 interest. Buying a 3-cost leaves 42, also 4. But rolling three times leaves 41, and one more puts you at 39 and costs you 1 interest every round until you climb back. This patch's top reroll plan, Invoker Teemo Alistar, slow rolls only with the gold above 50.

Do this instead

  • Before every roll, check you won't drop below a 10-gold breakpoint (unless you're doing your big roll-down).
  • Hit 50 gold by about 3-2 on a fast 8 game. Hold pairs on the bench instead of selling them.
  • Break econ only when your HP or the stage says so: under about 40 HP, or the planned roll-down stage.
Don't: Don't roll a little bit every turn. That's the most expensive habit in Gold.
