Leveling

Level 10 places 1.94. Level 8 places 6.64.

Gold sitting in the bank doesn't fight.

The principle

Levels raise your odds of finding higher-cost units and give you one more unit on the board. Fast 8 and fast 9 comps win by spending gold on XP at the right moment, not by rolling it away at level 7.

A real example (live data)

In Elderwood Ezreal Draven this patch, games that end at level 10 average 1.94, and games that end at level 8 average 6.64. Part of this is survivorship (if you live longer, you level more), but the gap is too big to explain away.

Do this instead

  • Decide your plan by 3-2: reroll comp (roll at 5/6/7) or fast 8 or 9. Then follow its level timing.
  • On a fast 8 plan, level 8 on 4-1 or 4-2 before you roll, not after.
  • If you're healthy (60+ HP) on stage 4 at 8, keep saving for 9 instead of rolling the gold away.
Don't: Don't roll at level 7 for 4-costs. Your odds are much worse than at 8.
