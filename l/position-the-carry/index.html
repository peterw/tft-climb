Positioning

Top players put Mama Beak in the back-row corner

Positioning wins the close fights.

The principle

Your carry has to live long enough to cast. Put it away from the threat: the back corner against melee, a hex in from the corner against assassins and hooks, and spread out against splash damage. Tanks go in the front row, facing the enemy's carry.

A real example (live data)

In Rapidfire Mama Beak, the most common spot for Mama Beak is the back-row corner. The comp page shows the full board.

Do this instead

  • Every round on stage 4+, look at where the enemy's carry stands and put your tankiest unit across from it.
  • Against assassins (they jump to your back row), move the carry one hex in and put a tank in the corner.
  • Against splash damage, leave a hex between your backliners.
Don't: Don't copy a fixed layout. Scout who you're fighting next.
