Leveling

3-star Azir: 3.45. 2-star: 5.86.

A reroll comp that doesn't 3-star is just a weak board.

The principle

Reroll comps give up the late game in exchange for 3-star cheap carries early. If you don't hit the 3-star, you're playing a weaker board at a lower level. Commit early, roll at the right level, and stop rolling once you hit.

A real example (live data)

Azir in Flora Fatalis Azir averages 3.45 at 3 stars and 5.86 at 2 stars.

Do this instead

  • Only reroll a unit if you hold 4 or more copies by 3-1 and nobody else is buying it.
  • Roll at the comp's level (5, 6 or 7) and keep 50 gold while you slow roll.
  • Once your carries are 3-star, stop rolling and level up.
Don't: Don't reroll a unit two other players are also buying.
