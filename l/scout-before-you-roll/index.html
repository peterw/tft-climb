Summoner Mama Beak drops 0.65 places when Fae Rengar is in the lobby

Scout before you roll.

The principle

The pool of each champion is shared by all 8 players. If someone else wants your units, your odds of hitting fall and your comp gets worse. It takes ten seconds to scout, and it changes what you should play.

A real example (live data)

This patch, Summoner Mama Beak places 0.65 worse on average in lobbies that also have Fae Rengar.

Do this instead

  • Scout the whole lobby at 2-1, 3-2 and just before your big roll-down.
  • If 2+ players share your carry, pivot to the comp that shares your items.
  • Before the final fights, position against your strongest opponent's board.
Don't: Don't lock a comp on 2-1 before you've seen the lobby.
