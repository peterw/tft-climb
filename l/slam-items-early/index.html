Slam an item on 2-1, even if it's not perfect

Gargoyle Stoneplate is the most-built item this patch.

The principle

A finished item on stage 2 wins rounds, which saves HP and earns streak gold. Holding components for the perfect item costs more than the item is worth. Flexible items like the ones below fit almost any board.

A real example (live data)

The most-built items in ranked this patch: Gargoyle StoneplateGuinsoo's RagebladeWarmog's ArmorSpear of ShojinJeweled GauntletSpirit Visage. They're popular because they fit almost any comp, so slamming one early is rarely wrong.

Do this instead

  • With 2+ components on 2-1, slam a flexible item.
  • Slam tank items first if you want to win-streak, carry items if you're open-forting into a reroll.
  • Use the carousel to finish a best-in-slot item, not to grab a random champion.
Don't: Don't keep 4+ components on the bench past 3-2.
