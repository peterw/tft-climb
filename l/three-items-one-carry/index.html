Stack items on one carry.

The principle

Items multiply each other. One carry with three items that work together beats the same items split across three units. Pick the carry early, even if it's a placeholder, and stack items there.

A real example (live data)

Master Yi in Adaptor Master Yi averages 4.10 holding 3 items and 5.67 holding none. Master Yi: 4.17 vs 5.65. Morgana: 3.94 vs 5.41.

Do this instead

  • By 3-2, pick one carry (the 'item holder') for your damage items, and slam on it.
  • Put tank items on one frontliner, not spread across all of them.
  • When you swap carries, move items with a Magnet or Reforger instead of rebuilding.
Don't: Don't slam damage items on a tank just because it's on the board.
