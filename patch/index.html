Systems: reforgers

Reforgers now have an internal queue system, preventing them from rerolling into the same items within multiple uses. For example, if you reforge a Bow into a Sword, the next time you reforge it, it cannot turn back into a Bow. This is a reforger buff and hopefully a frustration nerf.

Traits

Why Riot changed it

Coven: The highest breakpoint of Coven is getting more Essence per loss, so when you get that ultra high-roll Coven Lux, and some early Emblems, you can chase the deepest, darkest, Coven cashout of them all… eighth place.

Eclipse: As one of the highlights of the set as a semi-hidden trait with a quest to unlock, we want to make sure that it’s even more memorable in its final patch by making the executes come faster while delaying the moment that they start, making the Eclipse a more impactful and high-spectacle moment.

Elderwood: We added a new (and Legendary) unit to Elderwood, so we had to lower the (5) and (7) breakpoint’s power to account for how much easier (and more powerful) they will be to hit with end-game Ivern boards.

Fae: Fae’s final gold breakpoint was all but impossible to hit, but now it should be faerly possible given a very high-roll set-up. The final breakpoint is A TON OF GOLD by the way, so get chasing.

Inferno: Chasing the burning fantasy of vertical Inferno is now even hotter than before!

Primal: Primal Phoenix will now give players components faster, and more of them, through the course of the game.

Riftbeast: We’re lowering some of the power of the trait so we can put even more into Pebbles and Cinderling with the goal of bringing back one of our favorite reroll comps.

Sprykin: With Sprykin getting a new Yordle to the roster, the (7) piece is now accessible without a Spatula AND it has two powerful 5-costs in it. That’s a lot of power, so we’ve gotta trim some of the stats the trait provides to be properly Yordle-height.

AUTO SETTINGS: First up, we’ve introduced an Auto setting mode that lets TFT manage your settings. With Auto mode your settings may be switched from patch to patch to best match your device’s performance capabilities. You can swap out of “Auto” by switching to “Custom” settings. With 18.7 we've moved all players to "Auto" which will balance performance and visual quality for their devices. This impacts effects quality, resolution quality and frame rate caps, but not other settings like battery saver or performance mode on mobile

VISUAL QUALITY IMPROVEMENTS (MOBILE): Fixed lower than intended rendering resolution on select mobile devices. Default settings on some of these devices have been adjusted to 30FPS. Most devices should see visual quality upgrades. This will be most noticeable on Android, but does not cover 100% of devices. These visual quality improvement changes will be delivered to players on Auto mode, players on custom will not have their settings changed.

NEW RESOLUTION SETTINGS (MOBILE): We’ve decoupled resolution quality from the general quality setting so players have more agency to make tradeoffs on visual quality vs performance. Note that 100% on resolution quality does NOT guarantee full native resolution.

VARIOUS PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATIONS: We have made significant improvements across the game to make it run more smoothly for all devices, including significant hitching improvements for OpenGL devices in particular.
